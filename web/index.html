Drag across any chart to zoom in. Double-click or press Zoom out to go back. Short ranges show every 30-second window; longer ranges are averaged.

Where is the problem?

    Every hop is probed once per second. Routers often rate-limit replies to probes aimed at them, so loss at one hop only counts if it continues to every hop after it. Hops where it doesn't are marked "rate-limited".
